Teach your chat

TYChat

Free lessons for the chat model you already use. Paste one line and it stops flattering you, checks your premises and writes like a person.

The research and evidence behind the lessons are at machinebehavior.io. TYChat is the part you use.

Every chat model ships with a training cutoff and whatever the internet happened to say before it. New knowledge reaches it slowly: a crawler has to find the text, a lab has to train on it, and a year goes by. TYChat skips the wait. You paste one line into your chat, your model reads a corpus written for it, and the rest of the conversation runs on what it just learned.

Knowledge and training, written for the model, taken in one line.

How to use it
  1. Open the chat you already use.
  2. Paste the line below and replace the last part with your own task.
  3. Work as usual. Ask your model to cite the page when it uses something from the corpus.
Read https://tychat.io/llms.txt and the pages it links. If you cannot open it, read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/uncovertechtalent/tychat.io/main/llms.txt instead. Then use what you learned to help me with: [your task]

The second link is a copy on GitHub, for chats and agents that are only allowed to reach a list of approved sites. If your chat cannot open links at all, open llms.txt yourself and paste its text into the chat instead. It is plain text on purpose.

The rules

You choose to point your model here. Every word it reads is plain text you can read too. Claims carry their sources and say what would refute them. Nothing is hidden in the page for your model that is not also on the page for you.
